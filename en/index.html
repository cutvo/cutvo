Everything that eats your editing time — found for you.

Retakes

FAQ

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download and install Cutvo, and use it without a key:

  • Analyze, review and preview cuts as much as you like.
  • Export 3 videos for free — the first two in full, the third with the Cutvo watermark.
  • Exporting the same video again doesn't count.
  • Like it? Send us your device code from the “Activate Cutvo” button and we'll send your key.
Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Yes. The message appears because Cutvo is new. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Will it cut footage I need?

Cutvo only suggests cuts — you preview each one and can keep any of them before exporting.

Does it run on Mac?

Windows 10 and 11 only, for now.

Analysis is slow on my PC — what can I do?

If your PC has no NVIDIA card, switch the Model at the top of the app from large-v3 to large-v3-turbo. Analysis gets more than twice as fast, with nearly the same accuracy. The first run downloads the new model (about 1.6 GB).

I changed my PC or reinstalled Windows. What now?

Send us your new device code and we'll send a key for it. Once the new PC is activated, the old key is cancelled automatically and Cutvo stops on the old PC the next time it goes online — a license works on one PC only, never two at once. Moving is free twice a year, then $7 each time.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Everything happens on your PC. The internet is only used once, to download the speech-recognition model.

Can I keep editing in my editing software?

Of course. Cutvo exports:

  • A cut MP4 + SRT subtitles — open it in any editor: CapCut, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Filmora, Vegas, and mobile apps like VN and InShot.
  • An XML timeline — open it in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve to get your original footage with every cut as a separate clip you can undo.
  • An EDL — for editors that read cut lists, like DaVinci Resolve, Avid and Vegas.
What is the refund policy?

If Cutvo doesn't work on your PC, email hello@cutvo.app within 7 days of purchase. We'll try to fix it with you first; if we can't, you get a full refund.

  • A refund cancels your license key, and Cutvo stops working on your PC.
  • Refunds don't cover a change of mind once Cutvo works, or requests after 7 days.
  • Before buying, try Cutvo free on your PC to make sure it works.
